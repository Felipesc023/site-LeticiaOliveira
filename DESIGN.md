## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-end legal intelligence applied to property auctions (judicial and extrajudicial) and real estate law. The aesthetic draws from luxury editorial publishing, architectural restraint, and curated private wealth management. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Sovereign & Authoritative:** Instills immediate peace of mind for high-net-worth investors and buyers handling complex, high-stakes property acquisitions.
- **Understated Femininity:** Expressed not through superficial motifs, but via sculptural typographic contrast, organic warmth in earthy undertones, and immaculate composition.
- **Editorial Archival Quality:** Evokes tactile luxury—uncoated heavy linen paper, hot-stamped debossed typography, and silent precision.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Minimalism
Combining the disciplined spatial rigor of Swiss grid layouts with the sensory richness of contemporary haute couture editorial publications. The interface emphasizes expansive negative space, disciplined structural lines (1px hairline rules), zero ornamental clutter, and calculated asymmetric tension.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earth minerals, tactile paper hues, and architectural deep espresso tones. It steers clear of corporate navy and clinical whites in favor of an organic, timeless patina.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary (`#3D2B1F` - Espresso Profundo):** Anchors primary brand touchpoints, hero framing, primary call-to-actions, and structural architectural rules. It is applied selectively as an accent of authority rather than dominating page real estate.
- **Secondary (`#8C6249` - Avelã & Terracota Suave):** Used for micro-accents, active typographic states, category stamps, and subtle hover interactions.
- **Tertiary / Canvas Neutral (`#E8DFD8` & `#FBF9F6`):** Off-white base surfaces reminiscent of cold-pressed watercolor paper and raw natural linen. Forms the dominant atmospheric background across the platform.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#231F20` - Grafite Tinta):** Ensures high-contrast legibility for long-form legal articles, prospectus summaries, and contractual documentation without the visual harshness of pure black (`#000000`).

### Surface Hierarchy
- `surface-canvas`: `#FBF9F6` (Primary viewport canvas)
- `surface-subtle`: `#F4EFEA` (Alternative content bands and nested modules)
- `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Floating editorial cards and asset inspect panels)
- `surface-dark`: `#3D2B1F` (Selective focus sections, statement quote blocks, and footer foundations)
- `border-hairline`: `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.12)` (Universal 1px separator)

## Typography

The typographic strategy orchestrates tension between the dramatic, literary serifs of **Playfair Display** and the balanced, functional clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Titling:** Headlines utilize regular and medium weights in Playfair Display to maintain an authentic bookish grace. Avoid heavy bold serif weights, which compromise the tailored aesthetic.
- **Label Tracking:** All utilitarian labels, category stamps, kicker titles, and meta-data are set in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.25em`).
- **Body Rhythm:** Long-form legal analysis and advisory text must maintain generous line-height ratios (`1.6x` to `1.75x`) and moderate line lengths (max 65–72 characters per line) to optimize focus and comprehension.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an asymmetric, luxury editorial grid where whitespace functions as an active structural element rather than empty space.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid within a max-width container of `1320px`. Columns utilize 32px (`2rem`) gutters and outer margins of `80px` (`5rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px side gutters.

### Editorial Spacing Philosophy
- Major section transitions employ generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`), creating a tranquil, unhurried cadence.
- Asymmetry is leveraged intentionally: titles and asset summaries often offset to col-span 5, while accompanying legal contextual commentary spans col-span 6, separated by an intentional col-span 1 column of pure empty canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system discards heavy dropshadows, modern 3D lighting, and simulated physical extrusions. Depth is conveyed exclusively through tone, hairline boundaries, and surface overlapping.

### Elevation Principles
- **Zero Heavy Shadows:** Standard interface cards sit flush against the canvas, delineated strictly by fine 1px structural outlines (`rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.12)`).
- **Subtle Ambient Float (Modals & Sticky Navigation Only):**
  - Sticky Headers: Backed by `surface-canvas` at 94% opacity paired with a gentle backdrop-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a bottom hairline divider.
  - Due Diligence Panels & Drawers: A gossamer, diffuse ambient shadow:
    `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.08)`.
- **Tonal Layering:** Interactive states create depth by shifting background values (e.g., from `#FBF9F6` to `#F4EFEA`) rather than elevating the element along the z-axis.

## Shapes

The design system adopts an architectural, **sharp (`0px`)** shape language, reflecting the discipline of legal precision and classical publishing.

### Corner Rules
- **Buttons, Containers, Cards, and Modals:** Strict 0px border radius (sharp, crisp corners).
- **Images:** Always sharp 0px corners, framed with architectural aspect ratios (`4:5`, `16:9`, `1:1`) and optional 1px hairline boundary insets.
- **Form Controls:** Sharp 0px corners, reinforcing solidity and reliability.
- **Permitted Radii:** The sole exception is internal functional indicators (e.g., status indicator dots or circular icon avatars), which maintain an absolute circle (`50%` / `rounded-full`).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:**
  - Background: Deep Espresso (`#3D2B1F`).
  - Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans, uppercase, 12px, tracking `0.2em`, semibold, color `#FBF9F6`.
  - Padding: 16px vertical, 36px horizontal.
  - Border: 1px solid `#3D2B1F`. Corner radius: `0px`.
  - Hover: Background transitions to `#231F20` with smooth ease (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` in 240ms).
- **Secondary / Outline Action:**
  - Background: Transparent.
  - Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans, uppercase, 12px, tracking `0.2em`, color `#3D2B1F`.
  - Border: 1px hairline solid `#3D2B1F`.
  - Hover: Background fills with `#3D2B1F`, text shifts to `#FBF9F6`.
- **Editorial Text Action:**
  - Background: None. Border: None.
  - Styling: Inline text link accompanied by a delicate horizontal rule (`1px solid #8C6249`) that expands from left to right on hover.

### 2. Cards (Auction Dossier & Legal Specialization)
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Delicate Linen (`#F4EFEA`).
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.12)`.
- Padding: 32px to 40px internal spacing.
- Structure: Header tag set in `label-caps` (`#8C6249`), followed by an editorial serif headline, an optional statistical metric (e.g., "Deságio: 45%"), and an understated bottom divider leading to a detail link.
- Hover Effect: Subtle border color transition to `#8C6249` (no vertical lift or elevation shadow).

### 3. Chips & Status Badges
- Shape: 0px sharp rectangular tags.
- Sizing: 6px vertical, 14px horizontal.
- Typography: 10px uppercase, tracking `0.2em`, weight 600.
- Variations:
  - *Judicial:* Background `#F4EFEA`, text `#3D2B1F`, border 1px solid `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.2)`.
  - *Extrajudicial:* Background `#3D2B1F`, text `#FBF9F6`.
  - *Análise de Risco:* Background `transparent`, text `#8C6249`, border 1px solid `#8C6249`.

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- Styling: Architectural bottom-line or boxed outline format.
- Boxed: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.18)`, 14px 18px padding, sharp corners.
- Focus: Border shifts to `#3D2B1F` with zero outer glow rings.
- Placeholder: `#8C6249` at 50% opacity, Plus Jakarta Sans, regular weight.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Form: Precise geometric squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) with 1px hairline borders (`#3D2B1F`).
- Checked State: Solid `#3D2B1F` fill with an interior clean ivory pip or checkmark.

### 6. Editorial Dividers & Quotation Modules
- Separators: 1px solid `rgba(61, 43, 31, 0.14)` across section divisions.
- Editorial Pull-Quotes: Sized in `headline-md` (Playfair Display italic), accented by a single vertical 1px left accent bar in `#8C6249` with 32px inset padding.
